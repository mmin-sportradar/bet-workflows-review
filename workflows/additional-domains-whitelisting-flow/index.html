1 · SALES REPS · INFORMING SALES REPS

Approve the Request

Sales is informed regarding the request for additional domains whitelisting either:

  1. Directly by the client
  2. By Support via email

Sales needs to approve the request, even if request comes on the weekend or late night.

2 · SALES REPS · RE-OPENING JIRA

Re-open the Relevant Jira

In case client has more widget type solutions involved (e.g. LMT, H2H, Bet Assist), please re-open only one product relevant setup BETI Jira.

  1. Add domains in the comment section of the one selected Jira.
  2. Include the specific, predefined wording in your comment: “whitelist the following domain”.
  3. Assign it to the Bet-Integrations team.
  4. Set Jira status on “TO DO”.

For easier tracking, we suggest to always pick the Jira ticket with the lowest number (e.g. among Jira 535 for LMT, Jira 877 for Bet Assist and Jira 1122 for H2H, please update Jira 535).

3 · ADDITIONAL · URGENT / OFF HOURS

Unregulated Markets — Dummy Jira for Urgent Cases

For urgent requests on unregulated markets, if this happens during weekends or late hours, use dummy Jira BETS-10234. Save it on your phone and use it only if you are approving during these time periods, as Support will not accept approval without having a Jira ticket stated.

4 · CLIENT SETUP TEAM · DOMAIN WHITELISTING

Whitelist the Domains

Client setup team adds domains in 1–2 working days. Once complete, they inform the Sales Reps via comment in Jira ticket and close it.

Whitelisting process explained here.

5 · ADDITIONAL · URGENT / OFF HOURS

Submit the Urgent Request

If your request is urgent, you still need to request it via the respective Jira (Step 2).

Additionally, please forward the client’s email with domains list to Sportradar Support team along with Jira link and your approval. Explain the urgency.

6 · SUPPORT TEAM · URGENT / OFF HOURS

Whitelist Domains ASAP

Support whitelists domains as soon as possible in urgent cases.

7 · SALES REPS · FINALIZATION

Inform the Client

Sales rep may inform the client.

8 · SUPPORT TEAM · URGENT / OFF HOURS

Inform the Client

Support informs the client.

9 · ADDITIONAL · URGENT / OFF HOURS

Update the Client-Specific Jira

Even though you used dummy Jira for approval described in Step 2, you (or your SSS) still need to update client-specific Jira on the first working day (not the dummy one).

If you want your SSS to update the Jira, do not forget to put her/him in cc when you are approving during weekend.
