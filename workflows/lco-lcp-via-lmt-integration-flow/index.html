This document is intended for units involved in the technical integration process. *Steps for LCP via LMT only

1 · CS TEAM

Initiation

SF triggers a Jira Ticket for CS Team. CS Team starts a setup process in Betradar backend system, ISA and AVCMP.

CS Team assigns the ticket for requesting CDN setup at VTD team and checking correct client setup in AVCMP to AV Team (Matthias Maass).

AV content activation

Matthias informs AV-Admin team and the responsible AV-PSS about completed CS process.

AV-Admin team activates contracted AV content packages in AVCMP.

2 · BET INTEGRATION TEAM

Connecting the Products

Once the AV packages are set on AV side, AV team informs CS team about completed content activation.

CS Team clones the Jira ticket and assigns it to BET Integration team (BETI Project).

Check the availability of AV bookmakerID in Store. If AV bookmakerID is not set in Store, please connect AV bookmakerID with correct account.

Set additional parameter “LMT Highlights” to “TRUE”.

3 · BET INTEGRATION TEAM

Technical Instructions

Once everything is set on Sportradar Store, BET Integration Team passes the notice to AV team (Matthias) by Jira ticket and by e-mail (to av-integration@sportradar.com), along with the link to documentation for creating JWT token (needed for authentication of streams).

If RSA tokens are already set for LCO purposes, nothing additional is needed.

4 · AV TEAM

Informing the Client

AV-integration team contacts the client via e-mail and includes BET Integration team in the e-mail chain (bet-integrations@sportradar.com). E-mail sent to the client includes a link to technical documentation and a request for client to provide the Public RSA key (created in the process of obtaining JWT token) to BET Integration team (bet-integrations@sportradar.com).

AV integration team follows-up with client and BET Integration team until the client has gone live.

If RSA tokens are already set for LCO purposes, nothing additional is needed.

5 · BET INTEGRATION TEAM

Authentication Tokens

BET Integration team applies the provided Public RSA key to correct account.

If RSA tokens are already set for LCO purposes, nothing additional is needed.

6 · AV TEAM

Finalization

AV team (Matthias) opens a communication channel (preferably) on Slack, adding AV team, BET Integration team, responsible sales and client, for potential detailed integration and/or help with setting up correct parameters to existing LMT integration.
