Prior to LCO via LMT or LCP via LMT integration, the client needs an active LMT licence for all respective sports. LMT SF remains the same: Sales Reps add only LMT sports. AV PSS adds the LCO via LMT or LCP via LMT line to its Streaming Quote so the functionality is enabled on both ends (BET and AV).

AV TEAM

Streaming Quote and contract Add the LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT line and complete the AV commercial process.

STEPS FOR MTS CLIENTS

MTS BDM and BDA handoff Align the offer, Salesforce actions, and LMT licensing with the MTS teams.

1 · SALES REPS · PRODUCT OFFER

Product Offer

All selected sports are active

There is no action needed related to LMT administrative part. AV Team is responsible for the LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT line (refer to AV Team’s responsibilities).

Contact MTS BDMs for aligning and getting the offer.

Additional LMT sports needed

Get a separate pricing for respective LMT sports, using VBP on-line tool. Continue to step 2b. AV Team is responsible for the LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT line (refer to AV Team’s responsibilities).

To complete your offer, contact MTS BDM team to get info if price for additional LMT should be changed, depending on client’s payment model (MMF or Rev share).

A prospect does not have LMT

Get a separate pricing for respective LMT sports, using VBP on-line tool. Continue to step 2c. AV Team is responsible for the LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT line (refer to AV Team’s responsibilities).

To complete your LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT offer for agreed LMT sports, contact MTS BDMs to provide financial details if the rev share will be elevated with respective internal allocation to BET, or if the fixed fee will be applied.

e-leagues LMT is part of the e-leagues package. To get a price only for LMT e-leagues, please align with deal-desk.

2 · SALES REPS · SALESFORCE ACCOUNT INFO

Salesforce Account Info

Product selection

  • LMT
  • LCO via LMT
  • Virtual Stadium

All selected sports are active

No LMT Salesforce amendment is required. AV PSS creates a separate opportunity under the AV section for LCO via LMT or LCP via LMT and the respective sports.

Additional LMT sports needed · 2b

Amend existing LMT Opportunity to reflect agreed sports in LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT. Activate trial if needed. Continue to step 3b.

When you request additional sports, no need to send BET integration PDF doc to the client once the sports are set in Store, as client setup team will be in touch with client anyway in later phase to enable LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT functionality.

No LMT licence · 2c

Create a separate LMT Opportunity indicating agreed sports. Activate trial if needed. Continue to step 3c.

3 · SALES REPS · PRODUCT CONTRACT

Product Contract

All selected sports are active

For LCO / LCP sports and streaming content, AV PSS creates a separate contract stage and paper contract using AV templates.

Additional LMT sports needed · 3b

Amend existing LMT contract with new sports that would be a part of the LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT deal.

No LMT licence · 3c

Create a separate contract stage only for LMT and use Master Betting Agreement to reflect the product and sports.

AV TEAM

Streaming Quote and Contract

Product offer

For LCO / LCP sports and streaming content part, AV PSS Team gets a separate pricing via LMT integration type. Contact MTS BDMs for aligning and getting the offer.

Salesforce account info

Under AV section in SF, AV PSS team shall create a separate Opportunity selecting “LCO via LMT” or “LCP via LMT” product line with respective sports selected, indicating that integration type for selected streaming sports should be via LMT, add CDN traffic and make a Service Administration Request for LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT.

LCO via LMT / LCP via LMT product line triggers a Jira ticket for CS Team. Refer to LCO via LMT and LCP via LMT integration flow.

Product contract

Create a separate contract stage and paper contract using AV templates.

STEPS FOR MTS CLIENTS

MTS BDM and BDA Handoff

All selected sports are active

Contact MTS BDMs for aligning and getting the offer.

Additional LMT sports needed

Inform MTS BDAs to amend existing SF Opportunity for agreed sports and activate Trial if needed.

Inform MTS BDA team to finalize LMT SF process. Initiate LMT amendment signing and LMT sports licensing.

No LMT licence

Inform MTS BDAs to create a SF Opportunity for agreed sports and activate Trial if needed.

Inform MTS BDA team to finalize LMT SF process. Initiate LMT contract amendment where LMT will be added. Request LMT product licensing.
