ADDITIONAL · FREE TRIAL REQUIREMENT

Signing the amendment A signed amendment must be attached to the Jira ticket before BETI enables a Virtual Stadium Free Trial.

1 · SALES REPS · SALESFORCE ACCOUNT LEVEL

Salesforce Account Level

CREATE A NEW OPPORTUNITY with a Quote where the Virtual Stadium product is chosen (for the clients that don’t have a general betting contract);

or

AMEND OPPORTUNITY with a Quote where the Virtual Stadium product is chosen (for the clients that have a general betting contract).

2 · SALES REPS · JIRA SERVICE REQUEST CREATION

Jira Service Request Creation

Request the service / »Service Administration« button.

Define a function (Pre-Sales Trial, Post-Sales Integration).

This will then trigger an automatic process that will create a Jira ticket on the SF level and assign it to the responsible team:

– Jira ticket for the BETI team.

Trial phase lasts for 90 days and only one prolongation is possible (28 days).

3 · BETI TEAM · INTERNAL INITIATIVE / EPIC

Internal Initiative / Epic Jira Ticket Creation

When a Service Request ticket is received, BETI team needs to create a new Initiative / Epic in the BETI Jira Project; detailed instructions can be found here.

After the Epic is created, the automation will automatically create tickets for the following tasks:

  • Adapter
  • BETS Client Integration Prerequisites
  • Design
  • Custom Theming
  • BETI Client Integration Prerequisites (communication channel setup and other prerequisites)

4 · BETI TEAM · COMMUNICATION CHANNEL CREATION

Communication Channel Creation

BETI team will establish a shared Slack channel, including the Sales director and Sales support specialist for the initial setup phase.

Either Sales director or Sales support specialist will invite members from the client’s team to kick-off the setup process.

Both the SR technical team and the client’s technical team will be included in the channel to monitor the progress of technical development.

Requests to add external people to Slack channels can be made here.

5 · BETI TEAM · INTERNAL DEVELOPMENT

Internal Development

BETI team and engineering team (Dino Purić) will delegate the created tasks and monitor the progress, which is visible on the following Jira Board.

ADDITIONAL · FREE TRIAL REQUIREMENT

Signing the Amendment

To grant clients Virtual Stadium free trial, clients need to sign an amendment with a clause.

No commercials need to be included, if they are not agreed upon. If they are, they can be added to the amendment.

Signed Amendment (from client’s side at least) needs to be attached to the Jira ticket.

BETI team will only enable Virtual Stadium Free Trial if the Jira ticket has an attached signed Amendment.
