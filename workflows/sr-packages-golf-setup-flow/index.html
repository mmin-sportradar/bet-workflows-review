Premium Golf Service

Contracting and activation process

Responsibilities:
Important: AV streaming is available after mid-May 2026. Until then, Advanced AV-related setup is not required.
SPORTRADAR
3 September 2026

SALES

Set up the Salesforce opportunity

Premium Golf Service Essential

  1. Include PGS in the VBP opportunity, following the standard sales process and selecting the relevant product in the quote.
  2. Respect the revenue allocations from VBP.

Premium Golf Service Advanced

  1. Include PGS content in the VBP opportunity, following the standard sales process.
  2. Enter revenue only for the included products.
  3. Contact Streaming Sales using the details referred to in slide 2 of the source material.
  4. Do not share the contract with the client until the streaming part is aligned.

SALES

Complete contracting

All packages

  1. Ensure the client is contracted for all products included in the selected package offer.
  2. Use the MSA for contracting.
  3. For Premium contracts, add the Streaming appendix.
  4. Follow the normal process for quote approval and contract creation.

Advanced package

  1. Create one opportunity covering all products.
  2. Respect the revenue allocation from the Pricing Tool.
  3. At contract creation, place Streaming products in a child contract of the main Betting contract.
  4. Request and add the Streaming appendix to the paper contract for client signature.

SALES

Request the product trial

Request the product for trial in Salesforce through either a Tech Service Request or MTS Tech Service Request.

  • Use the normal / regular request process.
  • For the Essential package, activation is available and follows the normal approval process.

Emergency reactivation

  • Use emergency activation only to re-activate existing clients.
  • Do not use it to activate trial services or first-time services.

CLIENT SETUP TEAM

Activate after CS Jira

  1. Once a CS Jira is created, the Client Setup Team activates the product in ISA.
  2. The requester follows the regular process for Sox checks.
  3. Create a separate Jira ticket for LMT or LMT Premium Golf, then assign it to bet-integrations — or have the existing ticket reassigned to that team.

STREAMING

Coordinate Advanced AV setup

  1. Determine whether an AV integration period is required.
  2. If required, liaise with the relevant Streaming counterpart.
  3. The Streaming counterpart arranges a set-up call with the client.
  4. For an Advanced package, select AV CDN Usage.
  5. Use the default stated AV CDN Usage price of €0.10 per gigabyte.

Availability

AV streaming is available after mid-May 2026. Before then, these Advanced AV-related setup steps are not needed.

Manual communication

Communication between ECPs / SSS and Streaming Sales is manual; it is not automated in Salesforce.

INVOICE TEAM

Activate invoicing

Essential package

  1. Sales requests Invoice Activation through the regular process.
  2. After an SFINVOICE Jira is created, the Client Setup Team activates the product in ISA, or prolongs its end date.
  3. The Invoice Team triggers invoicing through the regular process.

Advanced package

  1. Sales requests Invoice Activation through the regular process.
  2. After an SFINVOICE Jira is created, the Streaming Setup Team activates the product in ISA, or prolongs its end date.
  3. The Invoice Team triggers invoicing through the regular process.
