## Additional Domains Whitelisting Flow
https://mmin-sportradar.github.io/bet-workflows-review/workflows/additional-domains-whitelisting-flow/
1. Approve the request — owned by SALES REPS
   Accept requests received directly from the client or from Support.
2. Re-open the relevant Jira — owned by SALES REPS
   Add domains and assign the selected product setup Jira to Bet-Integrations.
3. Use the dummy Jira — owned by ADDITIONAL · URGENT ONLY
   For unregulated markets during weekends or late hours, use BETS-10234.
4. Whitelist the domains — owned by CLIENT SETUP TEAM
   Add the domains within 1–2 working days, notify Sales in Jira, and close it.
5. Submit the urgent request — owned by ADDITIONAL · URGENT ONLY
   Use the respective Jira and forward the domains email, Jira link, approval, and urgency.
6. Whitelist domains ASAP — owned by SUPPORT TEAM · URGENT ONLY
   Support completes urgent domain whitelisting.
7. Inform the client — owned by SALES REPS
   Sales rep may inform the client once whitelisting is complete.
8. Inform the client — owned by SUPPORT TEAM · URGENT ONLY
   Support informs the client in urgent cases.
9. Update the client-specific Jira — owned by ADDITIONAL · URGENT ONLY
   Complete the client Jira on the first working day after a dummy-Jira approval.

FREQUENTLY ASKED
--------------------------------------------

## Which workflow do I need for my product?
Open the catalogue on the landing page and pick the product tile. Each tile lists only the routes that product actually has:

  - Setup flow — getting the product provisioned and configured for a client.
  - Integration flow — the technical work on the client side once setup is done.
  - Process flow — a standalone operational process, such as domain whitelisting.
  - Widgets licensing — the per-sport licensing route for Betting Widgets.

Every flow page then has a team switcher at the top. Pick your team and the diagram fades out every step somebody else owns, which is usually the fastest way to see whether a request is yours at all.

## How do I see only the steps my team owns?
Use the team switcher in the header of any flow page. Choosing a team fades every card owned by someone else, leaving your own path through the diagram at full contrast; All teams puts them back.

Your choice is remembered in this browser and reapplied on every flow you open afterwards, so you only pick it once. If you want to see the first-run prompt again, add `?coach` to the page URL.

One naming note: BETI appears as *BETI* on most flows and as *Integrations* on the widget ones — they are the same team, and the switcher follows your choice across both. Customer Care, Client Setup, Support, AV, CS and MTS are genuinely different teams and are never merged.

## What is the difference between LMT Plus, LMT Premium and LMT Virtualised?
They are three separate products in the catalogue, each with its own setup flow:

  - LMT Plus — the standard Live Match Tracker widget.
  - LMT Premium — the same tracker on a faster data path, for LMT and LMT Compact.
  - LMT Virtualised — the virtualised variant.

They share the LMT documentation but not the setup route, so start from the tile for the one the client bought rather than adapting another product's flow.

## A client's widget shows a licensing error on a new domain. What do I do?
That is the additional-domains process, not a product setup. Every domain a widget is embedded on has to be whitelisted against the client's licence — a domain that was never added produces exactly this error, including for staging and preview hosts, which are the ones most often forgotten.

Open the Additional domains process flow for who raises the request and who approves it. The troubleshooting page covers reading the specific error the widget prints, which tells you whether the domain is genuinely missing or the licence itself has lapsed.

## Why does SR Packages have a different flow for each sport?
Because the setup genuinely differs per sport. SR Packages is listed once in the catalogue, and opening it shows the sports first — Golf, Combat Sports, and E-Sports/E-League — each with its own setup and integration flow behind it.

Pick the sport and the dialog replaces the list with that sport's routes, so you only ever see the one you asked for. Everything else in the catalogue behaves the way it always has and goes straight to its flow.

## What is the difference between a setup flow and an integration flow?
Setup is everything that has to be true before the client can use the product: the commercial and provisioning path, service administration, product selection, licensing, and finalisation. It is mostly Sales, Client Setup and Customer Care.

Integration is the technical work once that is done: adapters, ID mapping, configuration and the client's own implementation. It is mostly BETI and the client's engineers.

A product with both listed has them as separate diagrams because they run at different times and involve different people. A product with only a setup flow either has no integration work worth diagramming, or it has not been drawn yet.

## Where is the documentation for a product?
Each product tile carries its own links, and there are usually two:

  - Technical documentation on apidocs.sportradar.com — how the thing works and how to call it. This is what you send a client's engineer.
  - Confluence on sportradar.atlassian.net — the internal page: ownership, commercial detail, and the history of the product. This one is for us.

The Product Hub tile under Operations is the central catalogue of everything Sportradar sells, which is the right place to start when the question is commercial rather than technical.

## What are the Utility and Mapping APIs for?
They are the two supporting APIs around the BET products rather than products a client buys on their own:

  - Utility API — coverage and availability questions: what a widget can show for a given match.
  - Mapping API — reconciling the client's own match and competitor IDs against Sportradar's.

Mapping is the one that turns up mid-integration, when a client's IDs do not line up with ours. The client setup flow covers who provisions access.

## How do I know a workflow page is still current?
Every flow page carries a Last updated stamp in its footer, shown as how long ago it changed — hover it for the exact date. The landing page has no stamp of its own because it is generated from the catalogue.

If a diagram disagrees with what actually happens, that is worth saying rather than working around: the flows are edited through the content admin and a correction is a small change.

## Are Bet Insights and Player Prop Zone the same thing?
They are two tiles that currently share one setup flow. Player Prop Zone used to sit inside Bet Insights and is now listed as a product in its own right, but the provisioning path is the same diagram, so both tiles open it.

The documentation is separate: Bet Insights and Player Prop Zone each have their own apidocs page and their own Confluence page, and those are the links to send on.
